### 1. AI Support & Refund Agent with Human-in-the-Loop

* **Title:** AI Support & Refund Agent with Human-in-the-Loop
* **GitHub Link:** [https://github.com/Dushmantha](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/Dushmantha) Herath1/support-ticket-agent
* **Description:** A deterministic customer support pipeline designed to eliminate LLM hallucinations through context preloading and enforce supervisor oversight on refund actions.
* **Main 5 Technologies Used:** LangGraph, FastAPI, Python, PostgreSQL, React 19
* **Main 3 Key Features:**
* Dual-model architecture utilizing Groq for high-throughput ReAct tool-calling and Gemini for customer-facing responses, ticket classification, and resolution audits.
* LangGraph PostgreSQL checkpoints with interrupt triggers to mandate human supervisor review on refund operations.
* Real-time asynchronous delivery of token streams and live tool-call statuses to a React 19 interface via FastAPI Server-Sent Events (SSE).

### 2. Crumb & Crust - Bakery E-Commerce & Operations Management

* **Title:** Crumb & Crust - Bakery E-Commerce & Operations Management
* **GitHub Link:** [https://github.com/Dushmantha](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/Dushmantha) Herath1/Crumb-and-Crust
* **Description:** A full-stack ordering platform and kitchen operations management system built to prevent production overbooking and ensure payment idempotency.
* **Main 5 Technologies Used:** FastAPI, React 19, PostgreSQL, Zustand, PayPal API
* **Main 3 Key Features:**
* Automated scheduling engine enforcing lead times, blackout dates, checkout-hold expirations, and daily capacity limits to prevent overbooking.
* PayPal Orders v2 integration with server-side capture verification and UUID idempotency keys to eliminate race conditions and duplicate transactions.
* Kitchen portal secured with OAuth2/JWT supporting date-based order filtering, real-time inventory toggling, and fulfillment tracking.
